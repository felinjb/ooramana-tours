The Itinerary

Four stops, one unhurried morning.

01

Kadamattom Church

Step into a 9th-century sanctuary where the legendary mystic priest Kadamattathu Kathanar is said to have bound spirits within these very walls. Trace the ancient Persian cross and hear tales passed down through generations of the parish.

45 minutes
02

Ooramana Temple

A short walk brings you to Ooramana's own heritage temple — quiet, unadorned, and deeply loved by the families who have tended it for generations. A moment to slow down before the village opens its workshops to you.

30 minutes
03

Traditional Pottery Making

Sit at the wheel beside village artisans whose families have thrown clay here for generations. Shape your own piece, feel the clay find its centre, and understand why this craft has never needed to change.

60 minutes, hands-on
04

Kerala Snack Making

Close the morning the way every Kerala household does — over hot tea. Learn to make pazhampori and unniyappam alongside a village family, then enjoy them fresh, still warm from the pan.

45 minutes
