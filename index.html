The Village

Ooramana keeps its own time.

Tucked beside the Muvattupuzha river, Ooramana has watched centuries pass without ever raising its voice. Persian traders once left a cross here. Potters still shape clay the way their grandmothers taught them. A mystic priest's legend still cools the afternoon air near an old church wall.

We built this trail so you could feel that instead of just reading about it — laterite underfoot, wood smoke in the air, and a village willing to let you in.
