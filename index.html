About the Village

Tranquil by nature, rich by heritage.

Tucked beside slow-moving backwaters near Kadamattom in Ernakulam district, Ooramana has kept its rhythm largely unchanged for generations. Temple bells still mark the hour, potters still shape clay by hand, and the same families that farm the paddy fields also welcome travellers into their homes.

This is village tourism built on relationships, not spectacle — a place where heritage architecture, living craftsmanship and everyday life sit quietly side by side, waiting to be noticed.
