About Ooramana

Nestled in the lush landscapes of Ernakulam district, Ooramana is a village where time slows down. Known for its deep-rooted heritage and vibrant local culture, it offers a rare window into authentic Kerala village life.

From the historic architecture of ancient temples and the nearby Kadamattom Church to the rhythmic sounds of local blacksmiths and potters, Ooramana is a living museum of traditions. Our tours are designed to connect you intimately with the land, the food, and the people.
