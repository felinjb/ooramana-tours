Frequently Asked Questions

Ooramana sits near Kadamattom in Ernakulam district, roughly an hour from Kochi. The easiest route is by road — we're happy to share exact directions and, where possible, help arrange local transport once your date is confirmed.

Light, comfortable clothing suits the climate best. Since the trail includes visits to a working temple and the historic Kadamattom Church, we ask guests to dress modestly — shoulders and knees covered — out of respect for these active places of worship.
