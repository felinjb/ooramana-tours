The Itinerary

Everything the village has to offer, in one day.

01–04

The Village Immersive

Your day opens exactly as our Half-Day journey does: Kadamattom Church and the legend of Kadamattathu Kathanar, the Ooramana Temple, hands-on pottery with local artisans, and a Kerala tea-time snack-making session.

Morning, ~4 hours
05

Muvattupuzha River Ride

Board a traditional country boat and drift along the calm waters of the Muvattupuzha river. Coconut palms lean in from both banks, egrets lift off ahead of the bow, and the village recedes into a green, quiet distance.

45 minutes on water
06

Rubber Plantation Tour

Walk shaded rows of a working rubber estate and watch traditional latex tapping and sheet processing up close — a craft that has quietly shaped this region's economy for over a century.

50 minutes
07

Authentic Village Feast

Sit down to a traditional Kerala Sadya — a full vegetarian feast of curries, pickles, and payasam, served fresh on a banana leaf exactly as it has been for generations. The day's fitting, unhurried close.

60 minutes
